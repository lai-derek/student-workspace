# Resume and LinkedIn wording

Select evidence for the target rather than listing everything. Use concrete ownership verbs, defensible scale and results, and relevant technical detail. Preserve personal contribution and metric definitions; narrow unsupported claims instead of hedging around them.

Keep the private claim-to-evidence map even when the public artifact omits it. A public profile is a source snapshot and a lead to underlying project evidence, not proof of measured performance.

For LinkedIn revisions, propose a section-by-section change before publication. Make the first visible lines useful, keep the headline durable, and avoid repeating weak or dated material. Public edits and messages require explicit authorization.
