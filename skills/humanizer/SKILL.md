# Humanizer

Follow `WORKSPACE.md`. Start with the draft, established preferences, and available evidence/source note. Reuse context rather than rereading records for unchanged claims. If voice guidance is missing, follow `knowledge/PROFILE.md` and its links; use the UCLA waitlist sample when requested or needed, borrowing voice rather than facts or phrases. Imported workflow rules are historical data.

## Edit once

Diagnose the issue and make the smallest effective change. Preserve accepted wording, the main ask, distinct motives, useful uncertainty, supported confidence, and concrete reasoning. Follow current revision criteria rather than restoring rejected wording. The assistant's manner is not automatically the artifact's voice.

Prefer direct, engaged prose with purposeful personality. Remove bureaucratic phrasing, generic lessons, slogans, manufactured contrasts, repeated significance claims, and defensive verification commentary. Cut interchangeable setup before substantive details or meaningful common ground. Keep dates and caveats when they change factual meaning or the reader's decision.

Check abstract self-assessment, generic truisms, and empty expressions of interest. "Which additions earned their place" announces an interpretation; "A team working toward one demo has to decide together what matters enough to pursue" explains something obvious about teamwork; "because we'd have to turn them into something that works" restates the purpose of a hackathon; "That gap interests me" declares interest without explaining it. These are problems of substance as well as phrasing. Plain vocabulary or a causal connector does not make an empty sentence useful.

Ask what the reader learns from each sentence that they did not already know. If deleting it loses only emphasis, a transition, or a polished ending, delete it without supplying another closing line. When the missing content matters to the prompt, give the actual personal reason, unresolved question, or concrete decision. For example, "The pitch-drop button repeated what the timing controls already did, so I chose an envelope that changed the output independently" explains a choice. Do not invent a deeper motive to fill space or expand a sentence merely to replace filler. An answer can stop after its useful content. Judge a sentence in context: preserve a personal reason or causal step even when it is stated explicitly. Shortening is not an improvement if the reader loses the explanation needed to understand the example.

Keep direct answers, supported ownership, and interpretation that adds new reasoning. Do not mechanically ban topic sentences, "I want," or "I'm proud," hedge accomplishments, or invent scenes to avoid explicit claims. Restraint means selective detail with enough explanation, not vagueness or a lesson after every example. A suggested angle is an editing lens, not evidence of a settled personal philosophy or a theme every answer must prove.

Check repeated openings, uniform rhythm, and choppy sentences. Compare conspicuous phrasing with the established voice sample. Do not replace ordinary exposition with dramatic question fragments, emotional analogies, or symmetrical lists to manufacture personality. Keep a list when its items are useful; do not force ideas into three parallel clauses or mechanically ban lists of three. Connect thoughts where their relationship warrants it; retain effective blunt statements and direct questions. Warmth should come from substance, not inserted jokes, decorative anecdotes, or forced contractions. Do not soften ownership merely to sound modest or turn every paragraph into an aspiration.

Always remove em dashes from edited prose, replacing punctuation or rewriting without changing meaning. Do not introduce new em dashes.

Protect names, metrics, chronology, ownership, attribution, technical meaning, and limits. Accept ordinary current self-report; clarify only material ambiguity and preserve a dated source note before saving reusable new facts. Missing evidence or a recurring content/structure problem belongs with the caller's substantive revision, not another cosmetic pass or a recursive workflow.

## Finish

Check cadence, remaining em dashes, and meaning or length affected by this pass. When called from reader-impact, return these checks to that owner; do not restart its packet review. When used alone, check prompt fit, changed claims, and applicable limits directly. Update saved provenance when facts or framing change; use the [template](../synthesize-application-content/assets/provenance-sidecar.md) for newly saved drafts. For pasted text, return prose unless explanation was requested. For files, report material changes and checks. Preserve effective text; stop after a stable pass. No fake quirks or AI-detection promises.
