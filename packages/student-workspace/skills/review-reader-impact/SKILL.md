# Review Reader Impact

Follow `WORKSPACE.md`. Establish audience, purpose, prompts, and limits. Critique only when requested; otherwise revise automatically. Reuse current context and ask only about consequential gaps.

## Read as the recipient

Scale review to the deadline and the size of the change. For substantial packets with enough time to apply feedback, use two fresh independent readers following [independent-readers.md](references/independent-readers.md). Review brief fields and routine follow-ups directly. When the user reports an imminent deadline, stop optional review rounds and deliver complete usable text to the requested destination. Do not wait for optional readers; check material claims and limits directly. Saving the text takes priority over further polish, screenshots, or record cleanup. Disclose direct review rather than simulating independence.

Assess the recipient's actual view before consulting private evidence. What do they understand and remember? What gives them reason to select, trust, or respond? Which passages lose attention, seem interchangeable, lack support, or assume missing context? Assess on-page support without inventing a selection rubric.

Read related fields together. Identify the overall impression the evidence supports and the distinct contribution of each answer. Check that the connection is understandable without repeating a thesis or forcing every interest into one theme. Each answer should add something useful while remaining understandable. Reusing a story is acceptable when it reveals something different; repeating the same lesson or result adds little. For outreach, ask why this recipient: if changing only the name leaves the message equally convincing, investigate missing common ground during editing.

## Strengthen the case

Prioritize consequential findings, not votes. A reader's question alone does not justify a caveat: identify the false or materially misleading claim before adding qualifications. A truthful amount raised needs no expense breakdown merely because a reader asks what was donated; an undefined "recent" needs no date solely to prove caution. Correct an actual raised-versus-donated error or explicit timing violation. Preserve useful dissent without scores, criticism quotas, or forced debate.

When edits are authorized, resolve consequential findings in the actual draft before reporting the review complete. A diagnosis is not the revision, and replacing one generic claim with another does not resolve it. Check the changed passage against the actual objection: did the missing explanation become understandable, did the generic claim gain supported substance, or did the framing change? A fuller word count or different phrasing alone does not establish a fix. Do not rerun a panel merely to approve routine fixes. If the missing substance cannot be recovered from existing evidence, ask for it rather than inventing it.

Now consult established evidence and source notes. Use [retrieve-application-evidence](../retrieve-application-evidence/SKILL.md) only for a specific gap or clearly stronger example. Check recipient cues against relevant linked personal records, including interests or plans outside the career topic. Replace a weak story when supported evidence materially improves the case; briefly explain the swap. Generated drafts are not evidence. Never invent facts to satisfy a reader's suggestion.

Unless the user's documented preferences say otherwise, aim for a clear claim, substantial evidence, concise interpretation, and purposeful personality. In selection writing, treat underselling as a defect: retain accomplishments, results, supported ownership, and actual reasoning. Accurate collaborative attribution is not a reason for apologetic framing. Mentoring may need a concrete question more than an achievement-led opening. Ask whether the text makes a memorable case or merely describes a competent applicant.

Preserve accepted phrasing, direct opinions, and the central question. Keep distinct motives and unresolved choices legible. Check repeated openings, uniform rhythm, and vague abstractions without forcing jokes, aphorisms, decorative scenes, or extended introspection. The assistant's conversational manner does not automatically define the artifact's voice. If criticism repeats, reconsider substance and structure before another polish.

## Finish once

Revise substantive issues, then use [humanizer](../humanizer/SKILL.md) at most once if needed. This review owns final checks; callers do not repeat them. This skill owns the final check of prompt coverage, all limits, changed claims and ownership, and packet consistency after editing. Reuse any checks the humanizer already completed rather than rerunning them; the caller only verifies transfer/save and later changes. For formatted resumes, use the existing PDF checks when layout changes. Follow the caller's saving convention and update the source note when needed; chat drafts need no files.

Reuse prior review findings on follow-up edits. Check the changed passage and affected context; repeat independent readers only for a material change to the case, audience, or packet, or when requested. Preserve effective text. Do not restart the full pipeline or add process commentary simply because the full draft is shown again.

Return the requested writing, briefly explaining consequential changes when useful; paste-ready requests need no routine recap. Critique-only requests receive prioritized findings. No submissions, outreach, or external-profile edits.
